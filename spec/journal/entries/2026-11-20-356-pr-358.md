# Let hosts declare which controls must stay unchanged for a guided click

A rotating promotion could invalidate a Checkout click even when the order had not changed.
Checking only the button would miss a change to the selected rows. [#358](https://github.com/team-poem/cairn/pull/358)
lets the host declare the controls the click depends on in `pageGuidanceScope` and opt in for
one observation with `freshness: "declared-scope"`. A click without an exact reference can then
proceed if its target is still unique and actionable and the driver can verify that its prerequisites
are unchanged. Whole-observation comparison remains the default; exact-reference actions keep their
existing checks.

In the Chrome/MCP cart fixture, both `discover()` and `explore()` clicked Checkout once while the
promotion rotated. Adding a second Checkout button or changing a row's checked state resulted in
zero clicks. If Chrome may have clicked but the driver cannot confirm it, the loop stops without
retrying or attributing the new page to an earlier step.
