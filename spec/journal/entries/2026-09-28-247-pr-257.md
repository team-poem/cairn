## What

A decision selecting the full product name `Wireless Earbuds Pro 2 $129.00 free shipping 4.8 (1,204)` by ref may now include `text: "Wireless Earbuds Pro 2"`; policy, execution and the frozen target still receive the complete canonical name. Contradiction feedback identifies the selected canonical element so the next decision can recover.

Consumer perception identity, role, duplicate ordinal, expired-reference and exact-node checks remain unchanged. Reference-binding trace gates retain their fixed messages.
