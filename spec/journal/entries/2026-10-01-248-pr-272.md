# Support inconclusive custom checks

_Drafted from [#272](https://github.com/team-poem/cairn/pull/272) by solp721. Edit this in place; do not delete and re-add it._

## What

Allow custom checks to return `{ inconclusive: true, detail }` when they cannot judge. Console and suite reports distinguish these checks from pass/fail, with trace support in v1.8.

## Why

Missing evidence should not count as success or an app regression. Inconclusive checks are excluded from pass proof and cannot trigger outcome healing or authorize a saved repair. Runs with no judged checks fail closed; existing custom check returns remain supported.
