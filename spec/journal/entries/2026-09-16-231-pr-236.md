# Share initialization and bound perception failures

_Drafted from [#236](https://github.com/team-poem/cairn/pull/236) by amazon7737. Edit this in place; do not delete and re-add it._

## What

Share Chrome driver initialization across concurrent first calls and make a pending transport available to `close()`. Stop splitting global perception failures into repeated measurements while retaining mixed-frame and stale-element isolation. Add independent coverage for the benchmark publisher's candidate repository filter.

## Why

A cold `observe()` could start three MCP processes and leave overwritten transports unclosed. An eight-candidate measurement rejected globally could trigger 15 probes. Initialization now shares one attempt, and a global rejection stops its batch after one probe. Failed startup remains retryable; losing an established browser session remains terminal.

The classifier accepts MCP's actual `Error:` envelope prefix. Failed evaluations are never retried with a different wait mode.

Refs #231 — addresses items **1, 2 and 4**. Item **3** remains deferred: iframe/shadow rows still cause the whole capture to withhold exact refs; per-document guards require separate design.
