# Capture evidence at selected execution checkpoints

_Drafted from [#328](https://github.com/team-poem/cairn/pull/328) by solp721. Edit this in place; do not delete and re-add it._

## What

Add opt-in `evidenceCapture` for replay, discovery and recovery. Selected captures finish before the next action, with run-local provenance and validated action-prefix bindings.

```ts
evidenceCapture: {
  checkpoints: [
    { id: "editor", after: editorPath, elements: true },
  ],
}
```

- Keep `onStep` synchronous and reuse existing screenshots at shared boundaries.
- Separate capture policy from required verification. Missing required evidence stays inconclusive and prevents green.
- Reject unsupported bindings safely, preserve secret redaction, and include capture in run/workflow budgets.
- Bump judgment revision to 2 and trace version to 1.14.

## Why

Final confirmation does not prove that an intermediate screen was correct. Hosts need evidence from the intended execution boundary without racing later actions.

Capture deadlines reject late results but drain in-flight driver calls; they do not forcibly interrupt an unresponsive driver.
